# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Plant directors, heads of electrical/maintenance, and energy champions at energy-intensive Indian manufacturers (typically Band A plants with large monthly power bills). They work in bright control offices and plant floors during day shifts, under pressure to cut energy cost and demand charges without ripping out EMS, SCADA, or PLC stacks. They need the next action, not another dashboard.

## Product Purpose

Stamped Energy is a read-only operational decision layer: it turns plant telemetry, application systems, and market data into ranked, rupee-scored prescriptions operators can accept or reject, then verify with evidence. OpenMontage in this repo produces brand motion and hero visuals (including the plant-flow hero) that sell that story for stamped.work and GTM. Success looks like a visitor instantly understanding “plant signals in → Stamped → prescriptions out” without EMS/MES cosplay.

## Brand Personality

**Direct · operational · evidence-backed.**

Voice is plant-office confident: plain industry language, ₹ impact up front, no neon “AI theater.” Emotion: calm urgency and competence. The interface should feel like a decision tool a plant head would trust at mid-morning, not a consumer SaaS launch page.

## Anti-references

- Purple-on-white SaaS gradients and glassmorphism defaults
- Hero-metric stat strips and identical icon-card grids
- Generic AI neural-net / circuit-board stock imagery
- EMS/MES/CMMS/plant-OS dashboard cosplay
- CVector clone kits (borrow structure only; never their marks, metrics, or claret/mindaro as brand primaries)
- Dollar-scored prescriptions as default (use rupee)

## Design Principles

1. **Show the loop, don’t narrate the stack.** Plant → Stamped → prescriptions should be visible in one composition.
2. **Practice plant-office light.** Demo-deck surfaces and Forge Coral accents; dark and impact-lime only as intentional scene modes.
3. **₹ is the argument.** Money and action copy stay readable; jargon expands into plain industry language.
4. **Operators stay in control.** LIVE/prescription chrome informs; it never implies PLC write or automated plant control.
5. **Inherit the website stack.** Space Grotesk / Inter / IBM Plex Mono and Forge Coral are identity, not up for “freshness” swaps.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast on light demo-deck surfaces. Honor `prefers-reduced-motion` with static or simplified reveals (no reliance on motion alone for meaning). Avoid conveying status by color alone when coral chips carry data; keep type size and weight hierarchies strong for secondary screens and hero chrome.
